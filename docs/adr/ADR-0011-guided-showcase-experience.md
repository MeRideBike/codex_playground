# ADR-0011: Guided showcase before multi-user identity

## Status

Accepted

## Decision

Prioritize a clear, fully usable guided showcase over authenticated-user work. Present reviewed mission templates as scenario cards, make the human approval point explicit, and progressively disclose raw audit data after a user selects a workflow stage.

## Consequences

First-time users can demonstrate ForgeBoard's central value—bounded engineering work with observable human control—without understanding internal template IDs or raw JSON artifacts. The UI does not claim identity, team collaboration, or repository authority that the application does not yet provide. Authentication remains deferred until the showcase workflow and related product boundaries are sufficiently clear.
